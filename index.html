About Fleekart

Building a global community where every useful item finds a second life.

Fleekart is a sustainable circular platform where pre-loved items keep going around instead of ending up in landfills. We connect people and local communities to make the transaction seamless, transparent, and rewarding — giving everyday items a meaningful second life while reducing carbon footprints.

Why Fleekart Exists

Every year, millions of perfectly functional items — from books and electronics to home goods and apparel — sit idle in storage or get thrown away. Meanwhile, someone nearby is actively searching for those exact items.

Fleekart solves this mismatch by replacing traditional re-commerce friction with a cashless, community-driven ecosystem. We believe sustainability shouldn't be complicated; it should be as simple as passing something along to a neighbour.

Earn Plinks

What are Plinks?

Plinks are the digital credits used on Fleekart to make every exchange happen!

How they work: Donors collect Plinks when they share pre-loved items, and Beneficiaries use Plinks to claim items they need.

Zero Money Involved: Plinks keep exchanges simple, safe, and fair — no cash, no pricing arguments, and no risk of getting cheated.

Never Expire: Your Plinks stay in your wallet forever until you're ready to use them!

How to earn Plinks

Welcome Bonus
Get 3,000 free Plinks as soon as you sign up so you can start claiming items right away!
Give Useful Items
Look around your house for clothes, toys, books, or gadgets you no longer use. Post them on Fleekart and collect Plinks as soon as someone grabs them.
Invite Your Friends (1,000 Plinks)
Earn 1,000 Plinks for every friend who signs up on Fleekart using your invite link or code — and they get 1,000 too, released once they complete their first exchange or 14 days pass.
Share a "New Life Story" (500 Plinks)
Loved an item you picked up? Post a quick photo or story on your profile and share it on social media to earn 500 Plinks, up to 1,000 a month.

Top-Up Option

Need a few extra coins to grab something special? You'll soon be able to purchase additional Plinks directly in the app for a small fee.

Impact

Did You Know?

Indian cities generate over 62 million tonnes of waste annually, with over 70% ending up in overflowing landfills. More than half of this is dry waste — including millions of functional items that could easily enter a local circular loop instead of a garbage dump.

  • 62 million tonnesof waste a year, from Indian cities
  • 70%ends up in overflowing landfills
  • Over halfof it is dry waste
  • 35%shorter product lifespans

Rising Consumerism & Disposable Culture

With e-commerce, instant deliveries, and trend-driven shopping, buying has become effortless. In India's growing urban centres, households buy significantly more products than previous generations, turning durable items — like clothing, toys, home décor, and small appliances — into short-term consumer goods.

Shrinking Product Lifespans

The active lifespan of everyday products has fallen by over 35% in recent years. Items aren't necessarily breaking down — owners simply move on to newer models, updated styles, or upgraded versions long before the product stops working.

The "Idle Household Storage" Trap

Before an item ever reaches a landfill, it sits in a closet, drawer, or storeroom for months or years. Over time, as it stays hidden away, it loses relevance, degrades in quality, and eventually gets tossed into municipal dry waste during home cleanouts. In urban India, billions of rupees in functional household items are discarded or sit idle without ever entering a circular loop.

“Every useful thing kept in circulation matters.”

Fleekart breaks this linear “buy-use-dump” cycle. Most items don't reach the end of their life — they just reach the end of their first owner's interest. By connecting users to give pre-loved items a second life, we keep good products working and prevent unnecessary waste, thereby saving raw resources and reducing carbon emissions.
