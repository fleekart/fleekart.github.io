About Fleekart

Good things shouldn't become waste just because you don't need them anymore.

Most useful objects stop moving long before they stop working. Fleekart is where they keep going — a Donor gives what they no longer need, a Beneficiary grabs what they do, and Plinks carry the value between them.

No cash changes hands. Nothing is listed for a price and nothing is sold.

Clothes, toys, books, gadgets. Anything still useful to somebody else.

What are Plinks?

Plinks are the coins used inside Fleekart. Donors earn Plinks when they give useful items. Beneficiaries use Plinks to grab useful items. They are not money and nothing is priced in cash.

What moves

Not just gadgets. Anything still useful.

Fleekart is a circular-use platform, not a resale site. The things that move through it are the ordinary, good things people stop needing.

Impact

Every useful thing kept in circulation matters.

A thing that keeps being used is a thing nobody had to make again. That is the whole argument for Fleekart: fewer good objects thrown out, more of them still doing their job somewhere else.
