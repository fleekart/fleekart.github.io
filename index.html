How it works

Four steps from a cupboard you never open to somebody who will actually use the thing.

1

List it

Three to five photos, the condition as it really is, and the Plinks you'd like for it. Pricing guidance helps where we have enough data.

2

Find things nearby

Browse within 2 km by default, up to 50 km. Listings show an area and a distance band — never anyone's exact address.

3

Request and talk

Send a request. Once the seller accepts, chat in the app and plan a pickup. Calls go through a masked number, so nobody's phone number is shared.

4

Hand over with a code

At pickup, the buyer shares a one-time code. The seller enters it, the buyer confirms receipt, and the Plinks move. Simple, and hard to fake.

Plinks are not money.

They are the community's way of keeping exchanges fair. You cannot buy them, cash them out, or move them outside the app.

Built for meeting strangers safely

Every part of the exchange is designed so you share as little as possible, as late as possible.

Your address stays yours

The pickup point is visible only inside an accepted exchange, and it is deleted 90 days after the exchange closes.

Adults only

Fleekart is for people aged 18 and over. Date of birth is checked at signup.

Report and block

Report from any listing, profile, request or chat. A serious report pauses the other person's new exchanges while we look.

Meet in public

We recommend daytime handovers at busy public places. Our Community Guidelines have the full checklist.

Every exchange counts

Each completed exchange shows an estimated CO₂e and waste saving, so you can see what keeping things in use adds up to. These are indicative estimates, not audited carbon accounting.
