1. About Fleekart

Fleekart ("Fleekart", "we", "us") is a platform on which people list items they no longer use and exchange them with other people nearby, in return for Plinks, a non-monetary credit inside the app. We are based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Legal entity details will be added here when incorporation completes.

Fleekart is a marketplace between users. We are not a party to any exchange. We do not own, inspect, store, deliver or guarantee any item. We do not handle money between users.

4. Plinks

Plinks are not money. They have no cash value, cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn, redeemed, transferred outside the app, or exchanged for money or goods anywhere except through a Fleekart exchange. They are not a deposit, a prepaid instrument, a security, a voucher or a currency. Fleekart does not hold funds on your behalf.

How Plinks work in this version of the app:

  • Starter grant. A new account receives 3,000 Plinks once, after the mobile number is verified. One number, one grant.
  • Wallet cap. Discretionary Plinks — the starter grant, invitation rewards, story bonuses and gifts received — cannot take your balance above 10,000. Plinks you earn by completing an exchange are never refused.
  • Exchange settlement. When a seller accepts your request, the agreed Plinks are held from your wallet. When you confirm you have received the item, the Plinks move to the seller and 10% of the exchanged amount is permanently retired from circulation. Retired Plinks are not Fleekart's revenue. Nothing is retired if an exchange is cancelled, fails or is reversed.
  • No-shows. If a confirmed no-show is recorded against you, the held Plinks are released and a penalty of 50% of the agreed amount is deducted from you. If your balance is short, the shortfall is recorded and recovered from Plinks you receive later.
  • Invitations. When someone you invite completes a verified signup, you each receive 1,000 Plinks, held until the new user completes their first exchange or 14 days pass without abuse, whichever is first. Invitation rewards are capped, and same-device or same-network pairs do not qualify. Sharing a link alone earns nothing.
  • Gifts. You can gift Plinks to someone you have exchanged with, up to 5,000 per day and 20,000 per calendar month (Indian Standard Time). The recipient's account must be at least 7 days old with one completed exchange.
  • Story bonus. An approved New Life story earns 500 Plinks, up to 1,000 per calendar month.
  • Pricing. Sellers set their own Plinks ask, up to 10,000 per listing. Where we show a suggested range it is guidance only.

Every Plinks movement is recorded in a ledger that is not edited afterwards; corrections are made as new, visible entries. We may adjust, reverse, cancel or withhold Plinks that were obtained through abuse, error or a breach of these terms, and we may change the figures above with notice in the app. Plinks that are the subject of an open dispute stay held until the dispute closes.

5. Listings

  • List only items you own and have the right to give away, that you actually have with you, and that are located where you say they are.
  • Describe the item honestly: real photos of the actual item (three to five), the true condition, and any defect. "For parts" items must say what is wrong.
  • Do not list restricted items: medicines; opened or expired cosmetics; unsafe electrical goods; weapons; live animals; counterfeit goods; hazardous materials; recalled products; stolen property; anything illegal to possess or transfer in India. The list is maintained in the Community Guidelines and may grow.
  • Do not put phone numbers, external contact details, off-platform payment requests or money prices in a listing.
  • A listing is reviewed before or after publication and may be held, edited, hidden or removed. A listing expires after 60 days unless relisted, and its media is purged 30 days after that.
  • Publishing a listing is an invitation to request the item, not a promise to any particular requester. A seller may decline any request.

6. Exchanges

  1. A buyer sends a request. A seller accepts one request; the buyer's Plinks are held.
  2. The two of you plan a pickup in the in-app chat, or through a masked call. The pickup point you agree is visible only to you two.
  3. At handover the buyer shows a one-time pickup code. It is valid for 30 minutes and can be regenerated. The seller enters it, then the buyer confirms receipt.
  4. Plinks move, 10% is retired, the exchange is complete, and both of you can rate each other.

Either side can cancel before handover; repeated cancellations and confirmed no-shows are penalised. If the code fails or something goes wrong at handover, either side can open a dispute and a Fleekart moderator decides on the evidence. Ratings are two-way and are not appealable; disputed conduct goes to the moderator instead.

The item passes between the two of you, as is. Fleekart gives no warranty about any item's condition, safety, legality, authenticity or fitness. Inspect the item before you confirm receipt. Once you confirm, the exchange is complete.

7. Conduct and safety

The Community Guidelines are part of these terms. In short:

  • Be honest. No fake listings, fake accounts, fake stories, fake ratings.
  • Be decent. No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, spam or scams.
  • Keep it on Fleekart. No asking for cash, UPI, or off-platform payment; no moving the deal off the app to avoid the rules.
  • No gaming Plinks: no self-dealing between your own accounts, no invitation farming, no collusion to farm bonuses.
  • No scraping, automated access, reverse engineering, or interfering with the service.
  • Meet safely: public place, daylight where possible, tell someone where you are going. In an emergency call 112.

Masked calls are not recorded by us. You must not record them without the other person's consent where the law requires it.
