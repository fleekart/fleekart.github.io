      <p class="lead rv">Fleekart is a sustainable circular platform where pre-loved items keep going around instead of ending up in landfills.       <div class="about__why rv">
        <h3 class="d3">Why Fleekart Exists</h3>

        <p>Fleekart solves this mismatch by replacing traditional re-commerce friction with a cashless, community-driven ecosystem. We believe sustainability shouldn't be complicated; it should be as simple as passing something along to a neighbour.</p>

        <div class="why__item">
          <h3>Rising Consumerism &amp; Disposable Culture</h3>
          <p>With e-commerce, instant deliveries, and trend-driven shopping, buying has become effortless. In India's growing urban centres, households buy significantly more products than previous generations, turning durable items — like clothing, toys, home décor, and small appliances — into short-term consumer goods.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="why__item">
          <h3>Shrinking Product Lifespans</h3>
          <p>The active lifespan of everyday products has fallen by over 35% in recent years. Items aren't necessarily breaking down — owners simply move on to newer models, updated styles, or upgraded versions long before the product stops working.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="why__item">
          <h3>The "Idle Household Storage" Trap</h3>
          <p>Before an item ever reaches a landfill, it sits in a closet, drawer, or storeroom for months or years. Over time, as it stays hidden away, it loses relevance, degrades in quality, and eventually gets tossed into municipal dry waste during home cleanouts. In urban India, billions of rupees in functional household items are discarded or sit idle without ever entering a circular loop.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="statement rv">
        <p>“Every useful thing kept in circulation matters.”</p>
        <span>Fleekart breaks this linear “buy-use-dump” cycle. Most items don't reach the end of their life — they just reach the end of their first owner's interest. By connecting users to give pre-loved items a second life, we keep good products working and prevent unnecessary waste, thereby saving raw resources and reducing carbon emissions.</span>
